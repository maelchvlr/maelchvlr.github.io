That One Otter Game

Unreal Engine 5 · C++ · Blueprints · Team of 7 · 2025

A mischievous co-op adventure where two young otters take on a wild rite of passage to prove their worth. Free on Steam, rated Very Positive with over 1,300 reviews, and shown in the Unreal Engine Student Showcase 2025.

This was my first Unreal 5 project, and it taught me Blueprints and C++ applied to Unreal. I was in charge of several technical challenges, including the camera that keeps two players in one fluid shot, and respawn. I still ship patches for the Steam version.
